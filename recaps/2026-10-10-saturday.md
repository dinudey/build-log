**Ethics and Trust — AI UX Ethics Analyzer** (started 2026-04-19, status: active but dormant for 6 months)

The experiment was built to answer two questions: can Claude analyze visual artifacts (wireframes, HTML prototypes) and produce ethics assessments that are actionable for UX designers? And can those assessments meaningfully distinguish between what a designer directly controls, what requires cross-functional buy-in, and what falls outside design's scope?
